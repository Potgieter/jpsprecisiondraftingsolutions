Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. While we are based in Gauteng, South Africa, we work remotely with clients globally using secure file transfer protocols.

What software formats do you support?

We primarily use SolidWorks but can export to STEP, IGES, DWG, DXF, PDF, and Parasolid formats to ensure compatibility with your system.

Can you sign NDAs (Non-Disclosure Agreements)?

Absolutely. We treat all client intellectual property with strict confidentiality and are happy to sign NDAs before project commencement.
